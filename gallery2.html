feature project

Interim

Interim: the purposeful pause between what was and what will be. A five-piece psychedelic rock band created in the heart of Bristol. Rather than revivalism, interim treat psychedelic rock as a loving form- structured, disciplined and cinematic. They’re pragmatic, focused and designed to keep things moving — stabilising teams, maintaining momentum and creating space for thoughtful long-form sets, built to be cohesive and textural. Inspired by the likes of King Crimson and Pink Floyd, Interim are driven to continue the mind-bending soundscapes of the golden era of music by delivering sets compromising soaring guitar work and entrancing rhythms. In short, an interim is not just a stopgap; it’s a strategic moment of possibility.
