About me

WHO AM I?

I am a music media student and aspiring live music photographer with a strong interest in documenting the energy, emotion and atmosphere of live performance alongside helping grassroots bands grow exposure. My journey into photography began as a way of combining my passion for music with visual storytelling, initially photographing small local shows and gradually developing my skills through hands-on experience in live venues.

What started as a creative outlet has evolved into a focused photographic practice, allowing me to explore how photography can preserve moments that are often fleeting. I am particularly drawn to capturing authentic interactions between performers and audiences, aiming to create immersive images that reflect the live music experience as a whole.

Through my work, I continue to build my portfolio and professional presence within the live music and events industry, with the goal of collaborating with artists, venues and promoters while developing my creative identity as a photographer.
